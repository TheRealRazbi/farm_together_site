How the numbers work

Profit
sell price − seed cost − 10, per tile, per harvest.
Profit / cost
Gold earned per gold spent on seeds. Useful early on, when money is tight.
Density
Profit per action (one plant + harvest cycle), on a log scale from 0 (least profitable crop) to 100 (most profitable). It shows how much gold each click gets you, whatever the growth time.
Gold/h per tile
"Nonstop" assumes you replant the moment a crop is ready. The other columns assume you only check in every 10 min, 30 min, 1 h, 8 h, or your own interval, so a crop waits until your next visit. For example, a 45 min crop checked every 30 min is harvested after 60 min. This is where fast crops fall behind: they're only great if you're actively playing.
Bars
Bar length is on a log scale within each column, so small values stay visible. The best crop in each column is in bold. Numbers of 1,000 or more are written as k (2.35k = 2,350).
